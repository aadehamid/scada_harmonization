## Evidence and authority

Dated supporting notes are in [session history](history/SESSION_LOG.md). Keep
historical claims qualified by their original session and evidence. Where the
record contains only a summary, it does not substitute for the owner's quoted
words when recording a new approval.

New decisions must include the owner's recorded words and identify the reviewed
proposal or version. Publication, file numbering, and a PR merge alone grant
no design approval. Open design questions remain open until that evidence exists.

## This reorganization

The owner's quoted permission and scope are in
